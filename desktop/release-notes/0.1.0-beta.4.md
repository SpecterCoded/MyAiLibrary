# My AI Library — Windows Beta 4

Turn videos, audio, documents, PDFs, notes, and research materials into an organized, searchable learning workspace.

My AI Library combines media playback, AI-powered search, study tools, source-aware answers, and local resource management inside one desktop application.

## What you can do

### Build your personal knowledge library

- Import videos, audio, PDFs, documents, notes, and research files.
- Organize resources into folders and playlists.
- Process multiple resources in the background with visible progress.
- Keep your original files connected to their transcripts, metadata, generated material, and research context.

### Ask questions about your content

- Chat with your entire library or ask questions about one resource.
- Receive source-aware answers with citations.
- Click timestamps to jump directly to the relevant moment in video or audio.
- Open cited media without losing your current conversation.

### Study and understand faster

Generate summaries, transcripts, chapters and subchapters, flashcards, quizzes, mind maps, notes, Active Recall exercises, and Self-Test questions directly from your content.

### Watch and listen with context

- Use complete video and audio playback controls.
- Browse searchable transcripts, captions, chapters, and subchapters.
- Save starred moments and review attachments and media metrics.
- Navigate directly between generated answers and the original media.

### Work your way

- Detach supported pages into separate desktop windows and attach them again.
- Keep multiple resources open using workspace tabs.
- Choose light, dark, or system appearance.
- Configure AI providers, models, storage locations, integrations, diagnostics, and updates.

## Local-first privacy and account security

Your library, processing data, logs, and application settings remain under your control on your computer. External AI services are used only when you configure them.

Persistent authentication refresh tokens are encrypted through Electron’s Windows-protected secure storage rather than being kept in browser storage.

This beta also strengthens account reliability in installed Windows builds:

- Firebase identity tokens are verified with Google’s JSON Web Keys.
- Signing-key rotation is handled automatically.
- Signup completes only after both email verification and local account creation succeed.
- Usernames resolve without case sensitivity.
- If an earlier local username mapping is missing, signing in once with the account email restores username login on that installation.

## Beta notice

This is a public Windows beta intended for testing and early feedback.

The installer is currently unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may display an **Unknown Publisher** warning. Windows code signing will be added in a future release.

Before testing beta software, keep a backup of important or irreplaceable files.

## System requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- 64-bit processor
- Internet access for account authentication, configured AI providers, and application updates

## Installation

1. Download `MyAI-Library-Setup-0.1.0-beta.4-x64.exe`.
2. Run the installer.
3. If Windows SmartScreen appears, review the warning and choose **Run anyway** if you trust the download.
4. Launch My AI Library and complete the initial setup.

The release also includes `beta.yml` for application updates, an installer blockmap for differential updates, and `SHA256SUMS.txt` for installer verification.
